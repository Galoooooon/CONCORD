Bug Report: Character stuck in wall on Level 3

GlitchMaster (Original Poster)

Whenever I try to dash near the eastern wall in the ruined cathedral on Level 3, my character clips through the geometry and gets stuck inside the pillar. I can't move or open the menu, and restarting the game is the only fix.

Replies (2)

JaneDev (Developer)

Thanks for reporting this! We're aware of the collision issue with the cathedral pillars. A hotfix is being tested right now and will be included in patch 1.0.4 launching later this week.

PlayerTwo

I had this happen too! A temporary workaround is to use your "Return" item if you have one equipped before you dash, it teleports you back to the checkpoint safely.
