Our Timeline

2023 — Concord was Founded

Founded in 2023, initially by a group of 4 student developers. For a web development project in their computer science class. The team continued the project beyond the classroom, leading to the formation of Concord Studios.

2024 — First Release

Concord released its first game in 2024, a simple puzzle game. Featuring a heart navigating through a maze of obstacles and challenges that players must overcome to avoid being left heartbroken in a cold and uncaring world.

2025 — Flagship Title

Alter Ego, a psychological narrative RPG game was officially launched in December 29, 2025. Leading to the studio's first major success. Featuring a high school student as the protagonist. Facing severe-depression, he decides dive in and find solace in the digital world.
